AI VIDEO

How to Create AI Videos for YouTube Shorts and Reels

Short-form video is one of the easiest formats for new creators to experiment with. AI can help with ideas, scripts, visuals, voiceovers and editing.

Step 1: Create a Strong Idea

Start with a simple concept that can be understood quickly. Funny stories, interesting facts, tutorials and relatable situations can work well for short-form videos.

Step 2: Write the Script

Keep the opening strong and remove unnecessary dialogue. The first few seconds should give viewers a reason to keep watching.

Step 3: Generate Visuals

AI image and video generators can be used to create characters, backgrounds and short cinematic scenes.

Step 4: Add Voice and Music

Add a suitable voiceover, background music and sound effects. Always make sure the final video feels natural and easy to follow.

AI PROMPTS

How to Write Better AI Prompts

A good prompt gives an AI tool enough information to understand exactly what you want. Better prompts often produce more consistent and useful results.

Describe the Subject

Clearly explain who or what should appear in the generated result.

Describe the Style

Mention the visual or writing style you want, such as cinematic, realistic, cartoon, professional or minimal.

Add Details

Include important information such as lighting, camera angle, environment, mood, clothing, composition or output format.

Use Negative Instructions

When supported by the AI tool, negative prompts can help reduce unwanted objects, visual errors and inconsistent results.
